Contact Web3Jobs

How can we help?

  • Report an expired or incorrect job.
  • Ask about a company or listing.
  • Request a correction or removal.
  • Ask about company job posting services.
  • Send a business or partnership inquiry.

Account support

If you already have a Web3Jobs account, use the support/contact option available inside the platform or the account area to submit your request.

For job applications, contact the hiring company through the application destination shown on the job listing.

Job corrections

Please include the job title, company name and listing URL when reporting an issue so that it can be reviewed efficiently.
