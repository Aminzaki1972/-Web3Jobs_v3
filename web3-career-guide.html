Web3 Career Guide

Web3 careers cover many disciplines, including software engineering, smart contracts, security, product, design, community, marketing, operations, research and business development. You do not need to be a blockchain developer to build a career in the industry.

1. Choose a career direction

Start by identifying the skills you already have. Developers can explore blockchain engineering and smart contracts. Designers can focus on product and user experience. Writers and marketers can work in content, growth and community. Finance and operations professionals can explore roles across Web3 companies and protocols.

2. Build evidence of your skills

Employers often value practical evidence. Build a portfolio, publish technical or industry writing, contribute to open-source projects, document useful experiments, or demonstrate relevant professional results.

3. Search strategically

Use job titles and skills together when searching. For example, combine blockchain with engineering, smart contracts, security, product, marketing or community. Check the company and application destination before submitting personal information.

4. Prepare for Web3 interviews

Research the company, understand its product and be ready to explain how your skills solve real problems. Technical candidates should be prepared to discuss projects and trade-offs. Non-technical candidates should be ready to explain measurable outcomes from previous work.

5. Stay safe

Never send cryptocurrency to a recruiter as a condition of employment. Be cautious with requests for wallet seed phrases, private keys, remote-access software or upfront payments. Verify job offers through the company's official career channels.
