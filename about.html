About Web3Jobs

Web3Jobs is a job discovery platform focused on Web3, blockchain and crypto careers. Our goal is to make it easier for candidates to discover relevant opportunities and for companies to reach people interested in the Web3 industry.

What we provide

  • Web3 and blockchain job discovery.
  • Search and filtering tools for career opportunities.
  • Company and job information in one place.
  • Tools for companies to publish and manage opportunities.

Job sources

Some opportunities may be supplied by external company career systems or job feeds. Web3Jobs may link candidates to an external application page. We do not guarantee that an external vacancy remains available, and candidates should verify important details with the hiring company.

Our approach

We aim to keep job information useful, accessible and clearly presented. We also work to remove or correct information when a listing is reported as inaccurate, expired or inappropriate.

Important

Web3Jobs is a job discovery service. It is not an employer, recruitment agency or financial adviser, and listing information should not be treated as an endorsement of any company or opportunity.
